How the adjustment works

Negative values indicate ingredients expected to support foam and therefore reduce the FRA target.

Positive values increase the FRA target.

Litre-based ingredients use a ppm/L coefficient when one is configured; ingredients without one are displayed only.

The coefficients are working/calibration values, not universal brewing constants. Calibrate them against your own beers and FRA product.
